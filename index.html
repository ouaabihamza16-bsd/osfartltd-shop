About us

An independent art company in London.

OSF ART LTD was incorporated on 10 May 2022 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 14096891. Based in London, the company exists to make original contemporary artwork and to share it with the people who care about it most: collectors, galleries and curators.

Our focus is the work itself. Some pieces are made as single, one-of-one works; others belong to series and editions that develop an idea over time. Either way, each piece is presented as art, with the idea behind it put first.

We keep the tone direct and plain-spoken, the way we think art should be talked about.

What we do

Four ways we work with art.

From the single piece to the longer series, and the people and places that bring art to an audience.

  • Original works

    One-of-one artworks, each made as a single, unique piece. Every work begins with its own idea and is developed until it can stand on its own, without needing anything else around it to make sense.

  • Series & editions

    Some ideas need room to grow. Series and editions let a theme develop over time, piece by piece, while each work keeps its own clear place within a defined set.

    SeriesEditions

  • Galleries & curators

    We welcome conversations with galleries, curators and venues interested in presenting work, whether it hangs on a wall or lives on a screen.

    Collectors are welcome to write to us about the work and the thinking behind it. We keep the conversation where it belongs: on the art.
